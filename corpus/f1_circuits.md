# Iconic Formula One Circuits

## Circuit de Monaco (Monaco)

Street circuit through Monte Carlo harbour. First held **1929**; F1 championship round since **1950**. Narrow, no runoff, **78** laps typically. Overtaking extremely difficult; pole position historically worth a win. Famous corners: Casino Square, Tabac, Swimming Pool chicane.

## Autodromo Nazionale Monza (Italy)

Temple of speed — **Temple of Speed**. Long straights through royal park; **average speed** among season's highest. Home race for Ferrari (**Tifosi**). Notable: Parabolica, Ascari chicane, Lesmo curves. Lowest downforce setups.

## Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps (Belgium)

**7.004 km** — longest current calendar track. Eau Rouge-Raidillon complex is iconic flat-out (conditions permitting). Weather unpredictable; rain often splits strategies. Jim Clark won four Belgian GPs.

## Silverstone (United Kingdom)

Hosted first world championship race **1950**. High-speed corners: Maggotts-Becketts-Chapel sequence. British motorsport heartland. Hamilton record wins at home circuit.

## Circuit of the Americas (USA)

Austin track designed by Hermann Tilke with elevation change into Turn 1. US GP revival cornerstone since **2012**.

## Suzuka (Japan)

Figure-eight layout; **130R** flat-out curve. Senna-Prost collisions **1989** and **1990** championship deciders.

Circuit characteristics dictate car setup: Monaco needs maximum downforce; Monza needs minimum drag; Spa balances both with weather risk.
